02

Live 2-Way Instruction

03

The 60-Minute Arc

Structure of an Ashray Session

A harmonious progression designed so you finish feeling revitalized and clear, rather than depleted.

Phase 1: Arrival & Joint Opening (10 Mins)

00 – 10 Min

Gentle breath check-in followed by Himalayan Sukshma Vyayama. Gentle neck circles, shoulder blade mobilization, and wrist warming to welcome the body into the day.

Phase 2: Fluid Alignment & Strength (25 Mins)

10 – 35 Min

Breath-led sequences focused on spinal decompression, hip opening, and graceful core engagement. The teacher gives continuous vocal alignment cues and tailored modifications.

Phase 3: Restorative Breath & Pranayama (15 Mins)

35 – 50 Min

Classical Himalayan breath control: Nadi Shodhana (alternate nostril breathing), gentle Sheetali, and resonant Bhramari to calm heart rate and settle cortisol levels.

Phase 4: Guided Stillness & Savasana (10 Mins)

50 – 60 Min

Supported relaxation and quiet contemplation, allowing your autonomic nervous system to absorb the benefits of practice and transition into your day with serenity.
