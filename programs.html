Specialized Modalities

Integrative Disciplines for Healing & Longevity

Each Ashray pathway weaves together classical elements of yogic science to treat the whole person.

🌬️

Pranayama & Prana Vidya

Breath is the conscious lever of the nervous system. Our faculty teaches authentic techniques preserved in Rishikesh lineages:

  • Nadi Shodhana: Balancing the sympathetic and parasympathetic nerves.
  • Ujjayi Breathing: Steady internal warmth and meditative concentration.
  • Sheetali & Sheetkari: Cooling breaths to soothe inflammation.
  • Bhramari: Resonant humming to activate the healing vagus nerve.
🧘‍♀️

Restorative Asana & Yoga Nidra

Cultivating an unshakeable inner refuge through guided dhyana (meditation) and conscious somatic stillness:

  • Bolster-Supported Asanas: Safe fascial release without joint pressure.
  • Guided Yoga Nidra: 45-minute psychic sleep for profound cellular reset.
  • Antar Mouna: Cultivating peaceful mental detachment and clarity.
  • Sankalpa Cultivation: Seeding conscious subconscious positive resolve.
🌸

Women’s Endocrine & Prenatal Care

Led by Dr. Priyamvada Joshi, bridging Ayurvedic gynecology with gentle yogic therapeutics:

  • Cycle-Attuned Practice: Modulating effort through follicular and luteal phases.
  • Pelvic Floor Integration: Restoring elasticity, core stability, and posture.
  • Gentle Prenatal Bloom: Trimester-specific asanas and calming breath.
  • Postpartum Rebuilding: Safe diastasis recti and spinal realignment.
