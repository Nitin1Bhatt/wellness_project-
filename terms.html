Terms of Engagement

Terms & Conditions.

Last updated: September 2026. Please read these guidelines regarding participation, practice safety, and cancellations.

1. Health & Physical Readiness

Yoga and breathwork involve physical movement, spinal articulation, and respiratory pacing. While our faculty provides therapeutic modifications and attentive supervision, participants are responsible for listening to their own bodies and honoring their physical boundaries. If you have an acute injury, medical diagnosis, or are pregnant, please consult your physician prior to commencing practice.

2. Punctuality & Live Studio Etiquette

To maintain the sacred quietude of our morning circles, participants are kindly requested to join the video session 3 to 5 minutes prior to the scheduled start time. Microphones are muted during collective flow to ensure clear audio transmission of faculty guidance.

3. Rescheduling & Cancellations

For Private 1:1 sessions, cancellations made with at least 12 hours prior notice can be rescheduled subject to calendar availability. For Intimate Morning Circles, sessions proceed on a set monthly weekday schedule.

4. Intellectual Property

All sequence materials, audio soundscapes, and instructional guidance provided by Ashray are protected intellectual property and are intended solely for your personal wellness practice.
