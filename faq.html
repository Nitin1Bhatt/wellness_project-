Frequently Asked Questions.

Everything you need to know about our teaching faculty, live format, international scheduling, props, and beginning your practice with Ashray.

Is instruction really 100% live, or pre-recorded videos? +
Every single session at Ashray is conducted live and guided in real time by our instructors over high-definition video. There are no pre-recorded video replays passed off as live classes. Your teacher watches your movements continuously to provide vocal adjustments, posture alignment checks, and personalized breathing pacing.
Who are the instructors at Ashray? +
Ashray is led by Ishani alongside a distinguished faculty of university-degreed and AYUSH-certified teachers, including Ananya Sharma (Pranayama specialist), Dr. Priyamvada (Therapeutic Anatomy & Iyengar Alignment), and Acharya Devraj (Yoga Nidra & Himalayan Meditation). You can review all teachers on our Instructors page.
I am a complete beginner with stiff joints. Is Ashray suitable for me? +
Yes, wholeheartedly. A substantial number of women who join Ashray have had no prior yoga experience or feel intimidated by fast-paced commercial studio classes. Because sessions are either private 1:1 or small circles of 5, the pace is patient, gentle, and strictly adapted to your present level of flexibility and comfort.
How does scheduling work if I live outside of India? +
We coordinate across international time zones every day. Many of our 1:1 students reside in the UK, Europe, the Middle East, Singapore, and North America. During your initial consultation, we discuss your local morning routine and align an agreeable regular slot with your chosen instructor.
What is the difference between Private 1:1 and Morning Circles? +
Private 1:1 Mentorship is entirely individualized: the sequence, pace, and lifestyle guidance are custom-crafted specifically for your body and goals. Intimate Morning Circles are limited to a maximum of 5 women practicing a shared morning flow together (Monday–Friday at 5:00 AM or 6:00 AM IST), offering a warm, accountable community at an accessible investment.
What props or equipment do I need at home? +
All you need to begin is a comfortable, non-slip yoga mat and a quiet, well-ventilated corner. If you have tight hips or hamstrings, having two yoga blocks and a simple cotton strap (or soft belt) is helpful. A laptop or tablet placed at waist height ensures your instructor has a clear view of your mat.
Are sessions conducted in Hindi or English? +
Our faculty is completely fluent in both Hindi and English. Sessions can be conducted entirely in English, entirely in Hindi, or in a warm bilingual blend depending on your comfort and preference.
What if I have an existing injury or medical condition? +
Please disclose any disc herniations, sciatica, cervical spondylosis, hypertension, or pregnancy during your consultation. Our faculty holds academic degrees in Yogic Science with a focus on therapeutic anatomy and will recommend safe modifications or advise if a specialized rehabilitative sequence is required before group practice.
How does the initial consultation work? +
The consultation is a quiet, 20-minute video or phone conversation. It gives you an opportunity to share your health background, schedule, and personal intentions, while we evaluate which instructor and format will best support your practice.
