In-Depth Study for Dedicated Students & Teachers.

6-Week Online Intensive

Foundations of Classical Himalayan Yoga

A comprehensive exploration of the Yoga Sutras of Patanjali, the Eight Limbs (Ashtanga), and how to bridge ancient philosophy with modern daily life.

  • ✓ Weekly 90-minute live lectures & Q&A led by Ishani
  • ✓ Textual study of Samadhi and Sadhana Padas with Sanskrit chanting
  • ✓ Reflective journaling assignments and personal sadhana guidance
  • ✓ Certificate of Completion upon coursework submission
