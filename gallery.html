Classical Asanas & Alignment Cues

Each posture balances spinal decompression, joint stability, and mindful breath connection.

Mountain Pose

Tadasana

Improves standing posture, balances weight distribution across the feet, and relieves lumbar compression.

Tree Pose

Vrikshasana

Builds nervous system balance, strengthens pelvic stabilizers, and encourages single-pointed drishti (focus).

Downward-Facing Dog

Adho Mukha Svanasana

Gently inverts blood flow, relieves hamstring tightness, and builds shoulder girdle strength.

Cobra Pose

Bhujangasana

Counters desk hunch, increases thoracic spine mobility, and gently expands lung capacity.
