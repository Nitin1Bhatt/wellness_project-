Brahma Muhurta: Why Practicing in the Sacred Dawn Transforms the Mind

In classical Ayurveda, the hour and a half before sunrise is characterized by Sattva guna — purity and natural quietude. Discover how waking before the world demands your attention restores hormonal balance and mental clarity.

By Ishani · Lead Faculty
Read Overview ▼
Brahma Muhurta represents the dawn window (~4:30 AM – 6:00 AM) when sensory stimulation is minimal. Practicing during this phase allows brainwaves to synchronize effortlessly into tranquil alpha frequencies.
Anatomy & Spine · 5 Min Read

Restoring the Cervical Spine: Daily Micro-Adjustments for Desk Workers

Prolonged forward head posture places up to 27 kilograms of additional strain on the cervical vertebrae. Here is how targeted thoracic extension and gentle jalandhara bandha relieve chronic upper back fatigue.

By Dr. Priyamvada · Anatomist
Read Overview ▼
Screen hunch causes hypertonic suboccipital contractions. Simple Sukshma micro-retractions and supported sphinx variations restore healthy lordotic curve and relieve cervical stiffness.
Breathwork Science · 6 Min Read

The Science of Prana: How Diaphragmatic Breath Calms the Vagus Nerve

Why shallow chest breathing perpetuates fight-or-flight chemistry, and how classical Himalayan Nadi Shodhana activates the parasympathetic brake to reduce systemic inflammation and emotional reactivity.

By Ananya Sharma · Faculty
Read Overview ▼
The vagal motor nuclei respond directly to lengthened exhalations. A 1:2 inhale-to-exhale ratio increases heart rate variability and clears adrenaline and cortisol.
Restorative Living · 4 Min Read

The Sacred Art of Savasana: Conscious Decompression in an Overactive World

Savasana is not a passive nap; it is the pinnacle of conscious surrender. Learn how ten minutes of deliberate stillness at the end of practice allows the nervous system to rewire and integrate the benefits of asana.

By Acharya Devraj · Meditation
Read Overview ▼
Savasana trains non-reactivity. Rather than falling unconscious into slumber, consciousness remains lucid while the physical body enters restorative physiological states.
