Guiding Faculty

Rooted in Pure Lineage. Dedicated to Your Well-Being.

At Ashray, our teachers are not performers on a stage. They are steeped in classical Himalayan traditions, certified in anatomy and Ayurvedic science, and united by a commitment to unhurried, personalized mentorship.

Founder & Lead Master

Ishani

Classical Hatha, Pranic Breathwork & Mindful Living
500-hr RYT Rishikesh AYUSH Certified 12+ Yrs Teaching

Founder of Ashray. Ishani spent over a decade studying beneath traditional Himalayan yogis in Rishikesh and Uttarakhand. Her teaching centers on nervous system down-regulation, compassionate postural calibration, and guiding women toward lifelong somatic calm.

Teaching Approach

Gentle, meditative, and non-performative. Focuses on breath-paced transitions and gentle somatic inquiry rather than aggressive flexibility.

Our Standards

Why Ashray Faculty Are Different

Zero Commercial Performative Pressure

Our instructors do not teach to social media trends. Sessions are confidential, reverent, and focused 100% on the internal state of the practitioner.

Continuous Clinical & Anatomical Review

All faculty participate in ongoing workshops covering spinal biomechanics, somatic trauma release, and Ayurvedic seasonal rhythms.

Strictly Limited Mentorship Numbers

Each instructor accepts only a strictly capped roster of 1:1 mentees and morning circles to ensure unhurried, energetic presence for every student.
